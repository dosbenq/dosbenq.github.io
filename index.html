    The watchdog that kept biting.

    Field crashes were slow to trace, so I wrote a Python tool that parses ADAS logs automatically. It led us to a critical watchdog-timer bug. Fixing it cut crash frequency by 50%.

    $500K secured. Then: road closed.

    I led a six-week proof of concept for an Intelligent Speed Assistance protocol suite, tied to the EU ISA mandate. My feasibility and build-vs-partner analysis won $500K in development funding. Then OEM timelines shifted and the program was shelved. Good analysis doesn’t guarantee the road stays open.

    Jan 2026 – now · Solo founder

    PointsMax: navigation for your points.

    Credit card points and deal-hunting are my side passion. I kept noticing how skewed hotel and airline loyalty can be: you pay full price and still don’t get the privileges the program promises.

    I was optimizing all of it by hand. I searched everywhere for a tool that did it for me and found nothing; everyone still does it manually. So I built one. PointsMax helps travelers who juggle five to eight loyalty programs see what a redemption is really worth at the moment they book.

    I built the beta from zero on my own: user research, product design, onboarding, backend architecture and the AI recommendation workflow.
